# Plans, billing, and usage

## To view and change your plan

1. Select **Plans & Billing** in the sidebar. Your current plan is named at the top and its card is highlighted.

   ***Figure 30.** Plans and billing* */}

2. Select **monthly** or **yearly** to switch the billing cycle. Prices update.
3. Review the plans. Each card lists what the plan includes and its limits.
4. On the plan you want, select the action button. What it says depends on your situation:
   - **Pay online** — pay now by card or another method. See [To pay online](#to-pay-online).
   - **Request this plan** — asks your provider to activate it. A banner confirms the request is pending.
   - **Switch to this plan** — appears for free plans.
   - **Request pending** — you already asked for this plan; wait for activation.

## To pay online

1. On the plan card, select **Pay online**. A menu of available payment gateways appears.
2. Select a gateway. Zprox.Chat supports **Cashfree**, **Razorpay**, and **PayU**, depending on what your provider has enabled.
3. Complete the payment in the checkout window.
4. You return to Zprox.Chat. A green confirmation appears and your plan is active straight away.

**Important:** You must verify your email address before you can pay online. See [To verify your email address](./Getting%20Started.md#to-verify-your-email-address).

**Note:** If a payment fails, Zprox.Chat returns you to the Plans page with a message. No plan change is made until payment is confirmed.

## To buy an add-on

Add-ons give you extra capacity on top of your plan, for example one more AI agent. They stack on the plan limit and carry their own validity period.

1. On the **Plans & Billing** page, scroll to the add-ons section.
2. Select the add-on you want, then select the pay action and choose a gateway.
3. Complete the payment. The extra capacity is active immediately.

**Note:** Add-ons are available only while you have an active subscription.

## Understand plan limits

Your plan caps things such as the number of AI agents, the number of automations, and the number of campaign messages per month.

- Panels for features your plan doesn't include still appear in the sidebar. Opening one shows a panel explaining the feature, with a link to **Plans & Billing**.
- When you try to create or use something beyond your limit, an **Upgrade your plan** dialog appears with the reason. Select **View plans** to go to **Plans & Billing**.
- Counters next to **AI Agents**, **Automations**, and **Forms** show how much of your allowance you've used.

## To review usage and costs

1. Select **Usage & Costs** in the sidebar.
2. Select **7 days**, **30 days**, or **90 days**.

3. Review the cards:

   | Card | What it shows |
   |---|---|
   | **Total spend (est.)** | Estimated total for the period. |
   | **WhatsApp** | Estimated Meta cost and the number of billable messages. |
   | **AI models** | Estimated model cost and the number of tokens used. |
   | **Average / day** | Estimated spend per day across the period. |

4. Review the charts:
   - **Daily spend** — estimated spend per day, split between WhatsApp and AI.
   - **WhatsApp by category** — per-message cost by Meta conversation category.
   - **AI by model** — token cost per model.

**Important:** These figures are estimates for planning. Your Meta and AI provider invoices are the authority.
