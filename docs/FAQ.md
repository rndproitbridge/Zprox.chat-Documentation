# FAQ

**Do I need a WhatsApp Business account to use Zprox.Chat?**
Yes, for WhatsApp. You need a Meta Business account with a WhatsApp Business Account and a verified phone number. See [Connect your WhatsApp Business account](./WhatsApp.md).

**Can I connect more than one WhatsApp number?**
Yes. Connect as many as your plan allows, and switch between them in the **Inboxes** panel in Chats and in the account picker in Lead Studio.

**Why can't I send a normal message to a customer?**
WhatsApp only allows free-form messages within 24 hours of the customer's last message. Outside that window, send an approved template. See [Understand the 24-hour messaging window](./Chats.md#understand-the-24-hour-messaging-window).

**How long does template approval take?**
Meta usually reviews a template within minutes, but it can take up to 24 hours. Zprox.Chat updates the status for you.

**Can I schedule a broadcast to send later?**
No. A broadcast either sends now or is saved as a draft for you to send later. To send on a clock, build an automation with a **Schedule** trigger. See [Block library reference](./Automations.md#block-library-reference).

**Can I use Zprox.Chat on my phone?**
Yes. The app adapts to small screens. Select the menu icon in the top-left corner to open the navigation drawer.

**Who can see a conversation?**
Admins see every conversation. Sales users see only the conversations assigned to them. Viewers have read-only access to the pages they're granted.

**What happens to a conversation an AI agent is handling when I reply?**
Select the robot icon in the chat header to pause the agent and take over. Select it again to hand the conversation back. See [To take over from an AI agent](./Chats.md#to-take-over-from-an-ai-agent).

**Where are my API keys stored?**
WhatsApp access tokens and AI provider keys are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM and are never shown in full after you save them.

**Can I import contacts from a Google Sheet?**
Yes. In Lead Studio, select **Import**, paste the Google Sheets link, and map the columns. See [To import leads](./lead%20Studio.md#to-import-leads).

**Do Instagram and WhatsApp share the same contact record?**
Contacts are stored per channel. Lead Studio can show every channel at once, or one account at a time.

**Can I send images or voice notes on Instagram?**
Not yet. File attachments, the media library, and voice messages are WhatsApp-only today.

**What's the difference between an automation and an AI agent?**
An automation follows the exact path you draw. An AI agent decides what to say using a language model. An automation can hand a conversation to an agent with the **Handoff to AI Agent** block.

**Why does my sidebar show fewer items than this guide describes?**
Either your role doesn't grant those pages, your plan doesn't include those features, or an app such as Shopify appears only once a store is connected.

**Are the costs on the Usage & Costs page my actual bill?**
No. They're estimates for planning. Your Meta and AI provider invoices are the authority. See [To review usage and costs](./Billings%20%26%20Usage.md#to-review-usage-and-costs).

**How do I stop messaging someone who asked to be left alone?**
The contact details panel shows an opt-out notice. Respect it — don't include that contact in marketing broadcasts.
