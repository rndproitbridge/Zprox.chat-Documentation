# Overview

## Target audience

This guide is for new Zprox.Chat users:

- **Workspace admins:** who set the workspace up, connect channels, and manage users, plans, and integrations.
- **Sales users:** who work the inbox, reply to customers, and manage their assigned leads.
- **Viewers:** who need read-only access to conversations and reports.

## What you need before you start

| Requirement | Why you need it |
|---|---|
| A web browser | Zprox.Chat runs in the browser. Use the current version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari. |
| A work email address | You use it to sign in, and to receive verification and password-reset codes. |
| A Meta Business account with a WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) | Required to send and receive WhatsApp messages. See [Connect your WhatsApp Business account](./Channels.md). |
| An Instagram **Business** account | Optional. Required only for Instagram DMs and comments. See [Connect your Instagram account](./Channels.md#connect-your-instagram-account). |
| An AI provider API key | Optional. Required only for AI agents. See [To connect an AI model provider](./Channels.md#to-connect-an-ai-model-provider). |

## Support and feedback

If you need help, contact your workspace admin first — they can change roles, plans, and channel connections for you.

For WhatsApp policy questions, such as the 24-hour messaging window, template rules, and account quality, see the official WhatsApp help centre at **[https://faq.whatsapp.com/](https://faq.whatsapp.com/)**.

To report a problem or suggest an improvement, contact PROITBRIDGE support.

# Features of Zprox.Chat

The key features of Zprox.Chat are:

- **Chats** — a shared team inbox for WhatsApp and Instagram direct messages, with live updates, media, replies, and per-account views.
- **Lead Studio** — your contact database, with groups, lifecycle stages, custom fields, lead scores, AI summaries, and CSV or Excel import and export.
- **Broadcast** — send an approved template or a text message to many contacts at once, with per-recipient variables and a delivery report.
- **Templates** — build WhatsApp message templates with headers, variables, footers, and buttons, and submit them to Meta for approval.
- **Automations** — a drag-and-drop flow builder with triggers, conditions, delays, actions, HTTP calls, Google Sheets, Gmail, and sub-flows.
- **AI agents** — let a language model answer WhatsApp messages and Instagram DMs and comments, call tools, and hand off to a person.
- **Forms** — build a lead-capture form, publish it as a public link, or send it inside WhatsApp.
- **Media** — a shared library of images, videos, audio, and documents that chats, agents, and templates can use.
- **WhatsApp and Instagram panels** — channel analytics, conversation starters, persistent menus, comment automations, and a comments inbox.
- **Shopify and WooCommerce** — order notifications, abandoned-cart recovery, cash-on-delivery confirmation, and coupons over WhatsApp.
- **Integrations** — Google Sheets and Gmail, AI model providers, Zoho CRM, inbound CRM webhooks, and MCP tools.
- **Plans and usage** — plan limits, online payment, capacity add-ons, and a spend dashboard.
