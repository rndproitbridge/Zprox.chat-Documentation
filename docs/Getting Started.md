## To create your workspace

Use this procedure if you're starting a brand-new workspace. If a colleague already created the workspace, ask them to add you as a user instead — see [To add and manage users](#to-add-and-manage-users).

1. In your browser, go to your Zprox.Chat address. The landing page appears.
2. Select **Get started**. The **Create your workspace** page appears.

   ![Figure 1. Create your workspace](/img/Assets/Figure%201%20-%20Create%20your%20workspace.png)
   ***Figure 1.** Create your workspace*

3. In the **Workspace name** box, type your company name, for example `Acme Inc.`
4. In the **Your name** box, type your full name.
5. In the **Work email** box, type your work email address.
6. In the **Password** box, type a password of at least 8 characters.

   **Tip:** Select the eye icon to show or hide what you typed.

7. If a captcha appears, complete it.
8. Select **Send verification code**. Zprox.Chat emails a 6-digit code to the address you entered.
9. In the **Verification code** box, type the 6-digit code.

   ![Figure 2. Enter your code](/img/Assets/Figure%202%20-%20Enter%20your%20code.png)
   ***Figure 2.** Enter your code*

   **Note:** The code expires in 10 minutes. If it doesn't arrive, wait for the countdown to finish and then select **Resend code**.

10. Select **Verify & create workspace**. Zprox.Chat creates your workspace, signs you in as the owner, and opens the **Plans & Billing** page so you can start a trial or choose a plan.

## To verify your email address

If your workspace was created for you, Zprox.Chat sends a confirmation link instead of a code. Until you use it, a yellow banner appears at the top of the app.

1. Open the email from Zprox.Chat.
2. Select the confirmation link. The **Verify email** page opens and confirms the address.
3. Select **Continue**. The banner disappears the next time the app checks your session.

   **Note:** If you verified in another browser tab, switch back to Zprox.Chat. The banner clears when the tab regains focus.

**Important:** You can't purchase a plan online until your email address is verified.

## To sign in to Zprox.Chat

1. In your browser, go to your Zprox.Chat address.
2. Select **Sign in**. The **Welcome back** page appears.

   ![Figure 3. Sign in](/img/Assets/Figure%203-%20Sign%20in.png)
   ***Figure 3.** Sign in*

3. In the **Email** box, type your email address.
4. In the **Password** box, type your password.
5. If a captcha appears, complete it.
6. Select **Sign in**. The **Home** dashboard appears.

**Important:** After several failed attempts in a row, Zprox.Chat temporarily locks the account. Wait for the lockout to end, or reset your password.

## To reset a forgotten password

1. On the sign-in page, select **Forgot password?**
2. In the **Account email** box, type your email address.
3. Select the send button. If the address belongs to an account, Zprox.Chat emails a 6-digit code.

   **Note:** For security, Zprox.Chat shows the same confirmation whether or not the address exists.

4. In the **Verification code** box, type the 6-digit code.
5. In the **New password** box, type a new password of at least 8 characters.
6. Select the confirm button. Zprox.Chat sets the new password and signs you out of all other sessions.
7. Sign in with the new password.

## To complete first-run setup

This procedure applies only to a freshly installed, self-hosted Zprox.Chat instance that has no users yet.

1. Open the Zprox.Chat address. The **First-time setup** page appears.
2. In the **Your name** box, type your name. This is optional.
3. In the **Email** box, type the email address for the owner account.
4. In the **Password** box, type a password of at least 8 characters.
5. In the **Confirm password** box, type the same password again.
6. Select **Create account & continue**. Zprox.Chat creates the owner account and signs you in.

   **Note:** The first user created this way becomes the workspace owner and has the **Admin** role.

## To sign out

1. At the bottom of the sidebar, select your name. A menu appears.
2. Select **Sign out**. Zprox.Chat ends the session and returns you to the landing page.

You can also sign out from **Settings** > **General** > **Account** > **Sign out**.

## The Home dashboard

**Home** summarises what happened in your workspace over a chosen period.

![Figure 5. The Home dashboard](/img/Assets/Figure%205%20-Dashboard.png)
***Figure 5.** The Home dashboard*

1. Select **7 days**, **30 days**, or **90 days** in the top-right corner to change the reporting period.
2. Review the metric strip across the top. It shows total contacts, new leads, open conversations, messages sent, response rate, and automation runs. Each number is a link — select it to see the detail behind it.
3. Review the working area:

   ![Figure 5.1. The Home dashboard working area](/img/Assets/Figure%205.1%20-%20Dasboard%201.png)
   ***Figure 5.1.** The Home dashboard working area*

   | Card | What it shows |
   |---|---|
   | **Lead funnel** | How many contacts sit in each lifecycle stage. |
   | **Contacts by group** | A donut of how your tagged contacts are distributed. |
   | **Automation performance** | Runs, success rate, waiting runs, and errors. |
   | **Recent broadcasts** | Your latest campaigns and their results. |
   | **Quick actions** | Shortcuts. Admins see **New Broadcast**, **New Automation**, and **New Template**. Sales users see **Open Chats** and **Contacts**. |
   | **Needs attention** | Warnings such as failed sends or expiring tokens. |
   | **Open tasks** | Tasks created by a human-handoff step. Select the circle to mark a task done. |

**Note:** The dashboard refreshes by itself about once a minute.

## To switch between light and dark themes

1. At the bottom of the sidebar, select the sun or moon icon.
2. The theme changes at once and is remembered on this device.

## To turn desktop notifications on or off

1. At the bottom of the sidebar, select your name. A menu appears.
2. Set the notification toggles:
   - Turn on the desktop-notification toggle to get a browser notification when a new message arrives. Your browser asks for permission the first time.
   - Turn on the mute toggle to silence notifications without turning them off.
3. Select anywhere outside the menu to close it.

**Note:** Notification settings are per user and per browser. If you sign in on another computer, set them again there.

![Figure 6. The user menu](/img/Assets/Figure%206%20-%20Settings.png)
***Figure 6.** The user menu*

## To set your timezone

Your timezone controls how times appear in reports, and when scheduled automations run.

1. Go to **Settings** > **General**.
2. Under **Timezone**, select the box. A searchable list appears.
3. Type part of a city or region name to filter the list, for example `Kolkata`.
4. Select your timezone. Zprox.Chat saves the change.

## To set up the 24-hour window reminder

WhatsApp lets you send a normal message only within 24 hours of the customer's last message. This setting defines the nudge Zprox.Chat sends before that window closes, so a conversation stays open.

1. Go to **Settings** > **General**.
2. Find the **24-hour window reminder** card.

   ![Figure 7. 24-hour window reminder](/img/Assets/Figure%207%20-%2024%20hour%20-%20Window%20reminder.png)
   ***Figure 7.** 24-hour window reminder*

3. In **Send this long before the window closes**, set how early the reminder goes out.
4. Under **What to send**, choose the message type:
   - **Text** — type the message to send.
   - **Template** — select an approved template from the list.
5. Select **Save**. The **Auto-remind** toggle becomes available in every chat.

**To use the reminder on a conversation:** Open the chat and turn on **Auto-remind**, or turn it on for many leads at once from Lead Studio. See [To run bulk actions](./Engage.md#to-run-bulk-actions).

## To create categories and groups

A **category** is a label type, such as `Interest` or `City`. A **group** is a value inside that category, such as `Pricing` or `Chennai`. A contact can hold only one group per category, which keeps your segments clean.

**To create a category:**

1. Go to **Settings** > **Category**.
2. Select **Add category**.
3. Type a **Name**, for example `Interest`.
4. Type a **Description**. This is optional.
5. Select **Save**.

**To add groups to a category:**

1. On the **Categories** page, select the category. The category detail page appears.
2. Select **Add group**.
3. Type the group name, for example `Pricing`.
4. Choose a colour. The colour is what you see on badges in Chats and Lead Studio.
5. Select **Save**.

**To delete a group:** Open the category, select **Delete** on the group row, and confirm.

**Important:** Deleting a group removes it from every contact that carries it. This can't be undone.

## To create lifecycle stages

Stages describe where a lead sits in your sales process, such as `New`, `Qualified`, `Won`.

1. Go to **Settings** > **Stage**.
2. In the box at the top, type the stage name.
3. Select **Add**. The stage appears in the list with a colour.
4. To change a colour, select the colour swatch on the stage row and pick a new one.
5. To rename a stage, select its name, edit the text, and press **Enter**.
6. To reorder stages, drag a row up or down. The order you set here is the order shown in the Home funnel and in Lead Studio.
7. To delete a stage, select **Delete** on the row and confirm.

## To create custom contact fields

Custom fields capture details that aren't built in, such as `Company size` or `Renewal date`. They appear when you edit a contact, and you can map form answers and imported columns to them.

1. Go to **Settings** > **Fields**.
2. Select **Add field**.
3. Type a **Field name**.
4. Select a **Type**:

   | Type | Use it for |
   |---|---|
   | **Text** | Short single-line values. |
   | **Text area** | Longer notes. |
   | **Number** | Numeric values. |
   | **Phone** | Phone numbers. |
   | **Email** | Email addresses. |
   | **Date** | Calendar dates. |
   | **URL** | Web addresses. |

5. Type a **Description**. This is optional and helps your team know what to enter.
6. Select **Save**.

**To edit or delete a field:** On the **Fields** page, select **Edit** or **Delete** on the field row.

## To add and manage users

**Admin only.**

1. Go to **Settings** > **Users**.
2. Select **Add user**.

   ![Figure 8. Add user](/img/Assets/Figure%208%20-%20Add%20users.png)
   ***Figure 8.** Add user*

3. Fill in the user's display name, username, email address, and a starting password.
4. Select a **Role**:

   | Role | What the user can do |
   |---|---|
   | **Admin** | Everything, including settings, users, plans, and integrations. |
   | **Sales** | Works only the chats and contacts assigned to them. |
   | **Viewer** | Read-only access. |

5. For a Sales or Viewer user, select the pages they may open, such as **Chats**, **Lead Studio**, or **Broadcast**. Clear a page to hide it from them.
6. Select **Save**. The user appears in the list.

**Other actions on the Users page:**

- **To disable a user:** Select their **Active** badge. They lose access immediately but their record is kept.
- **To edit a user:** Select **Edit** on their row.
- **To reset a password:** Select **Reset password** on their row.
- **To delete a user:** Select **Delete** on their row and confirm. Contacts assigned to them stay in the workspace but become unassigned in their view.

**Note:** You can't disable or delete your own account from this page.

## To delete your account

**Important:** This permanently removes your account. It can't be undone.

1. Go to **Settings** > **General**.
2. Under **Account**, select **Delete account**.
3. In the box that appears, type `delete`.
4. Select **Delete account** again to confirm.
